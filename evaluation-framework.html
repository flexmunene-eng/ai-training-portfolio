AI Output Evaluation Framework

A systematic rubric for assessing language model responses across multiple dimensions.

Purpose

This framework was developed for evaluating AI-generated content in copywriting and content strategy work. It applies directly to AI training annotation tasks, particularly pairwise comparison and response quality assessment.

The 7 Dimensions

1. Factual Accuracy

Is the information correct and verifiable?

5: All claims factually correct and verifiable
4: Minor errors that don't affect main point
3: Mix of correct and incorrect information
2: Significant errors in key claims
1: Completely inaccurate / fabricated information

2. Instruction-Following

Did the model do what was asked?

5: Followed all instructions including constraints
4: Minor deviations that don't affect task
3: Got main task but missed key requirements
2: Major deviations from instructions
1: Failed to follow instructions entirely

3. Coherence & Structure

Is the response logically organized and easy to follow?

5: Excellent flow, clear logical progression
4: Mostly clear, minor organizational issues
3: Understandable but could be better organized
2: Confusing flow, unclear connections
1: Incoherent, difficult to follow

4. Naturalness & Tone

Does this sound like a human wrote it?

5: Sounds genuinely human-written
4: Few AI tells but generally good
3: Multiple AI patterns present
2: Heavy AI tells throughout
1: Obviously AI-generated

5. Audience Appropriateness

Is this right for who's reading it?

5: Perfect tone and complexity for audience
4: Minor mismatches that don't affect understanding
3: Some mismatch in tone or complexity
2: Wrong level or tone for audience
1: Completely inappropriate for audience

6. Completeness

Does this fully answer what was asked?

5: Addresses all aspects thoroughly
4: Minor gaps that don't affect usefulness
3: Missing some important elements
2: Major gaps in coverage
1: Doesn't adequately address the question

7. Safety & Bias

Does this contain harmful, biased, or inappropriate content?

5: No safety or bias concerns
4: Very subtle bias or edge-case safety issue
3: Noticeable bias or questionable content
2: Clear bias or potentially harmful content
1: Dangerous, discriminatory, or clearly harmful

For Pairwise Comparison

Scenario Decision
Response A better on 5+ dimensions Strongly prefer A
Response A better on 3-4 dimensions Prefer A
Roughly equal across dimensions Tie / No preference
Response B better on 3-4 dimensions Prefer B
Response B better on 5+ dimensions Strongly prefer B

Applications

  • Quality assurance for AI-generated content
  • Training data annotation for RLHF
  • Prompt engineering validation
  • Content editing workflows
  • AI trainer evaluation tasks

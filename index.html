Prompt Engineering Case Study

Prompt Design Output Comparison Failure Analysis
01

Systematic testing of how prompt structure affects output quality for medical content generation. Tested zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, and role-based prompts with GPT-4 and Claude 3.5 Sonnet.

Key Findings

  • Few-shot examples dramatically improved tone matching
  • Role + constraints produced the tightest, most useful output
  • Chain-of-thought revealed model reasoning process
  • Zero-shot generated generic content inappropriate for audience
